## Urban Institute Education Data Portal — `data/schools-index.json`

`data/schools-index.json` contains 102,178 rows extracted from the Urban Institute
Education Data Portal, which republishes the U.S. Department of Education's Common Core
of Data in a harmonised form. It is generated by `scripts/build-school-index.mjs` and is
committed to this repository so that a clone builds and runs without a network call.

**Required citation**

> Common Core of Data school directory (2024), Education Data Portal (Version 0.26.0),
> Urban Institute, accessed July 31, 2026,
> <https://educationdata.urban.org/documentation/>, made available under the
> [ODC Attribution License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

**Licence** — [Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/)

ODC-By permits sharing, creating and adapting the database, including redistribution,
on the condition that attribution is given. It is the attribution-only licence in the
Open Data Commons family: unlike ODbL, it carries **no share-alike obligation**, so it
places no licensing requirement on the code in this repository.

The underlying Common Core of Data is a work of the U.S. federal government. The ODC-By
terms attach to the Urban Institute's database, not to the federal facts within it.

Endpoint queried:
`https://educationdata.urban.org/api/v1/schools/ccd/directory/2024/`

## Grants.gov

Funding opportunities are queried live from the Grants.gov Search2 API, operated by the
U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. No Grants.gov data is redistributed in
this repository.

## ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer

Foundation financials are queried live from the ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API,
derived from IRS Form 990 filings. No ProPublica data is redistributed in this
repository.
